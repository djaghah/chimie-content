ChemEN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

ChemEN (“the app”) is a chemistry practice game for students in grades 7–12, based on the Romanian school curriculum. It is made by an independent developer (Google Play developer: Djaghah).

In short

  • No accounts, no names, no ads, no analytics or tracking, no chat and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress (language, grade, stars, records) is stored only on your device, and in your Android backup if backup is turned on.
  • The app goes online only to check for new exercises, by downloading a public file from cdn.jsdelivr.net. It sends no personal data. Like any website, the CDN provider may keep technical logs (such as the IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data, and it asks for no permission other than internet access.

Children and students

The app is meant for students, including teenagers. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, no personal information of children is collected.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything the app has saved.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: bogdansarac@gmail.com.
